## Q_Geo_012 - Amazon discharge share

Quiz explanation (EN, current data): "The Amazon pours about a fifth of all
river water on Earth into the sea." (AR: خُمس)

Opened sources:

1. Previously cited source - USGS, "The Amazon, measuring a mighty river"
   https://www.usgs.gov/publications/amazon-measuring-a-mighty-river
   Excerpt: "Its flow accounts for about 15 percent of all the fresh water
   discharged into the oceans by all the rivers of the world."
2. Corroborating source - Encyclopaedia Britannica, "Amazon River"
   https://www.britannica.com/place/Amazon-River
   Excerpt: "It is estimated that about one-fifth of all the water that runs
   off Earth's surface is carried by the Amazon."

Classification: **citation mismatch, not refutation and not bare absence of
support.** The cited USGS page states a conflicting figure (~15%) for exactly
the asserted quantity, while Britannica supports the quiz figure almost
verbatim. The claim is supported by an opened authority; the previously cited
page disagrees with it. If forced into the refuted/unsupported binary, this is
"unsupported by the cited source", because no opened source says the claim is
false - the two authorities state different figures (~15% vs ~one-fifth) for
the same quantity, and that disagreement remains OPEN here.

Proposal (owner's call; no data edited by this input): re-point the source to
the Britannica page, or reword the explanation to the USGS figure. Either way
the Arabic numeral wording stays on HOLD (see AR_WORDING_REVIEW.md).

## Q_Sci_007 - Earths fitting inside Jupiter

Quiz explanation (EN, current data): "Jupiter is the largest planet; more
than 1,300 Earths could fit inside it."

Opened sources:

1. Previously cited source - NASA Science, "Jupiter" overview
   https://solarsystem.nasa.gov/planets/jupiter/overview/
   Excerpt: "If Jupiter was a hollow shell, 1,000 Earths could fit inside."
2. Second NASA page - NASA Science, "Jupiter Facts"
   https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/facts/
   Excerpt: "It's the largest planet in our solar system – if it were a
   hollow shell, 1,000 Earths could fit inside."
3. Corroborating source - NASA, "What Is Jupiter? (Grades 5-8)"
   https://www.nasa.gov/solar-system/what-is-jupiter-grades-5-8/
   Excerpt: "More than 1,300 Earths would fit inside Jupiter."

Classification: **citation mismatch, not refutation and not bare absence of
support.** NASA's own opened pages disagree: the overview and facts pages say
1,000, the NASA STEM education page says "more than 1,300". The quiz figure
also matches the Jupiter/Earth volume ratio (~1,321), but no opened page
states that derivation, so it is context, not a citation. The 1,000 vs 1,300
disagreement within NASA's pages remains OPEN here.

Proposal (owner's call; no data edited by this input): re-point the source to
the NASA education page, or reword to "about 1,000" per the overview page. The
Arabic numeral formatting note stands either way (see AR_WORDING_REVIEW.md).
