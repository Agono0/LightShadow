## 3. Full description

### 3.1 English (~350 words)

LIGHT SHADOW is a first-person educational horror game about the night
knowledge becomes light.

You are Nour, nineteen, climbing the fence of your late mother's school the
night before its demolition. Your goal is simple and human: carry her memory
box home. But the school has locked its last light away — inside fuse boxes,
inside paper, inside the things she left behind — and the Shadow, made of
everything students ever forgot here, wants the night to stay empty.

Every fact you speak aloud makes your flashlight burn brighter. Answer real
curriculum questions — science, math, history, geography, language, health —
and the Bright Idea Meter pushes your beam from four metres to twelve.
Hesitate, and the dark leans in.

There is no combat and no gore. The Shadow never touches you; it erases.
Posters go blank. Names come back wrong. A voice like chalk under a door asks
you to forget, politely. Your defense is knowledge and composure: restore fuse
boxes, recover the six fragments of the memory box, and say true things out
loud until the building remembers itself.

- **Real study, real stakes:** 60 curriculum-tuned questions with warm
  explanations; the ending you earn depends on what you actually learned —
  accuracy, fragments recovered, and how calmly you faced the dark.
- **A branching night:** 12 authored story moments, three hidden stats
  (boldness, trust, calm), timed choices where hesitation matters, and one
  shortcut only the brave find.
- **Four endings,** from Full Recovery to Dawnless — each with its own dawn.
- **A Shadow you outthink, not outgun:** patrol, stalk, chase — and repel by
  holding your focused beam. Walking, sprinting, crouching; no jumping, no
  weapons.
- **English and Arabic** (Egyptian-flavored Modern Standard Arabic) throughout,
  with display-time Arabic shaping.
- **Plays fully offline.** No ads, no accounts, no data collected.
- **Calm by design:** checkpoints, not punishments, and a study recap of what
  you learned that you can actually keep.

LIGHT SHADOW is made for students, families, and anyone who has ever gone back
for something they could not leave behind. Headphones recommended — the school
whispers.

Even the worst ending is quiet, not cruel: the school keeps what you carried,
gently, the way a library keeps a book nobody borrowed.

كل حقيقة تنطق بها بصوت عالٍ تجعل مصباحك يشتد نورًا. أجب عن أسئلة حقيقية من

## 6. Privacy policy blurb (short-form, for the listing + policy page)

**English.** LIGHT SHADOW is an offline, single-player game. It collects no
personal data: no account, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no
location, no contacts, no user-generated content. Save data (story progress,
settings, study recap) is stored locally on your device only and never leaves
it. The game requests no dangerous Android permissions; if device haptics are
enabled in a future update, the vibration control is used only for gameplay
feedback. There is no in-game chat or communication with other players. Crash
reporting, if ever added, will be strictly opt-in and disclosed here first.
Questions: contact the address listed on the store page.

**Arabic.** ضوء وظل لعبة فردية تعمل دون اتصال بالإنترنت. لا تجمع أي بيانات
شخصية: لا حسابات، ولا تحليلات، ولا معرِّفات إعلانية، ولا موقعاً، ولا جهات
اتصال، ولا محتوى من المستخدم. بيانات الحفظ (تقدم القصة، والإعدادات، وملخص
الدراسة) تُخزَّن على جهازك محلياً فقط ولا تغادره أبداً. اللعبة لا تطلب أي
أذونات أندرويد خطرة؛ وإذا أُضيفت الاهتزازات في تحديث لاحق فستُستخدم للتحكم في
الاهتزاز لأغراض اللعب فقط. لا توجد داخل اللعبة أي وسيلة محادثة أو تواصل مع
لاعبين آخرين. وإذا أُضيف تسجيل الأعطال مستقبلاً فسيكون اختيارياً بالكامل ويُنص
