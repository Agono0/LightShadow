# LIGHT SHADOW — Style Guide

Consolidated from what the project actually does today: `AGENTS.md`, the
`GameConfig.cs` field set, the real asset names under `Assets/_Project/`, and
the adopted master plan (`Docs/MASTER_PLAN.md` §4.10, §8.4, §9.1) as anchors.
Where the plan and shipped naming disagree, the shipped naming below wins
(`Docs/MASTER_PLAN.md` reconciliation header).

**Last updated:** 2026-11-19.

## 1. Naming conventions

English, PascalCase prefix + no spaces (AGENTS.md rule 3). Applies to every
team-made asset; anything under `Assets/ThirdParty/<pack>/` keeps its
original pack naming and is never renamed or edited (AGENTS.md rule 4).

| Prefix | Used for | Actual examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Mat_` | URP materials — generated scene surfaces and art PBR surfaces | `Mat_Wall`, `Mat_Chalkboard`, `Mat_Art_PlasterWall`, `Mat_Art_WoodFloor`, `Mat_Art_HandSkin` |
| `M_` | VFX / shader-driven materials (not plain PBR surfaces) | `M_Art_Beam`, `M_Art_DustMote`, `M_Art_SmokePuff`, `M_ShadowSmoke` |
| `SM_` | Static meshes (procedural or imported) | `SM_Wraith`, `SM_BeamCone` |
| `SK_` | Skinned meshes (none yet — reserved) | — |
| `ArtModel_` | Mesh-swap placeholder objects keyed by name in `SceneBuilderArt.ApplyArtPass`; renamed in place when the art pass lands | `ArtModel_…` in 02_School |
| `T_` | Textures | `T_PlasterWall_Albedo`, `T_WoodFloor_Smoothness`, `T_FlashlightCookie`, `T_ParticleSoft`, `UI_Backdrop` |
| `Q_[Cat]_[NNN]` | Question assets, zero-padded 3-digit index. Categories: `Sci` `Mat` `His` `Geo` `Lan` `Hea` | `Q_Sci_001`, `Q_Geo_010`, `Q_Hea_002` |
| `SFX_` | One-shot/loop SFX; variants take `_0.._N` | `SFX_Click`, `SFX_QuizCorrect`, `SFX_QuizWrong`, `SFX_Pickup`, `SFX_Power`, `SFX_DoorCreak`, `SFX_FootstepConcrete_0..3`, `SFX_FootstepWood_0..1`, `SFX_ShadowMoan_0..2` |
| `MUS_` | Music files (reserved — music is currently procedural) | — |
| `AMB_` | Ambience loops | `AMB_SchoolNight` |
| `SN_` | Story node assets (`StoryDirector` graph, D4) | `SN_Act1_01`, `SN_Act2_01_calm` |

### Material sub-patterns (as generated by the pipeline)

- PBR art surfaces: `Mat_Art_<Surface>` (`Mat_Art_PlasterWall`, `Mat_Art_MetalLocker`, …).
- Tiled variants generated per object scale: `Mat_Art_<Surface>_T<W>x<H>`
  (`Mat_Art_PlasterWall_T9x3`, `Mat_Art_GateMetal_T6x1`) under
  `Materials/Art/Tiled/`.
- FBX-import remapped materials from the packs: `Mat_Shared_<8-hex>`
  under `Materials/Art/Shared/` (Kenney pack materials converted to URP/Lit
  at import).

### PBR texture suffixes

Poly Haven sets import as `T_<Surface>_<Suffix>` under
`Textures/Art/<Surface>/`:

- `_Albedo` (base colour JPG)
- `_Normal` (from Poly Haven `_nor_gl`)
- `_Roughness` (from Poly Haven `_rough`)
- `_Smoothness` — generated twin PNG (roughness inverted), under
  `Textures/Art/Generated/`; the plan's `_BaseColor`/`_MaskMap` suffixes from
  §7.4 are NOT used in this repo.

### Tuning fields

All tunables live in `GameConfig.asset` (AGENTS.md rule 2) as camelCase public
fields, grouped by system — `meterMax`, `meterGainCorrect`, `meterDrainPerSecond`,
`lightRadiusMin/Max`, `questionTimer`, `batterySeconds`, `walkSpeed`,
`sprintSpeed`, `crouchSpeed`, `enemyPatrolSpeed/StalkSpeed/ChaseSpeed`,
`sightRange`, `catchDistance`, `repelMeterThreshold`, `repelCooldown`,
`sprintNoiseRange`, `caughtMeterPenalty`, `dawnStalkRadius`. No magic numbers
in code: a new tunable gets a `GameConfig` field, not a literal.

## 3. Audio levels and import targets

Anchors from the adopted plan §8.4 (enforced once real recorded music/VO
lands — Phase 1/P5):

- **Dialogue (VO):** normalize to **−16 LUFS**.
- **SFX:** peaks at **−3 dBFS**.
- Short SFX: mono, ADPCM, decompress on load; ambience/music: Vorbis 60–70,
  streaming; voice: Vorbis, compressed in memory.
- Category mixing today: code-level `VolumeCategories`
  (Master/Music/Ambience/SFX/UI, PlayerPrefs-backed — D15); a real
  AudioMixer + snapshots replaces it in Phase 1 (`Docs/TASKS.md`).
- Silence is a weapon: ambience sits low; the school-night loop
  (`AMB_SchoolNight`) is the only bed under gameplay.

## 4. UI / touch specs

- **Landscape only**, both orientations, safe-area aware layouts.
- Touch targets **minimum 64×64 dp** with **12 dp spacing** between the right
  cluster buttons (plan §9.1); joystick dead zone 10%.
- One shared sprite sheet aesthetic: `UI_Panel`, `UI_Button`, `UI_Glow`,
  `UI_Backdrop` (Textures/UI/).
- All user-facing strings go through `Localization` with EN and AR both
  filled; Arabic shaped at display time (`ArabicSupport`, `Localization.CurrentFont`).
  Bundled fonts pending Phase 1 — until then Arabic relies on OS fonts.
- Controller art style anchor (plan §9.2): brass rim, worn chalk-white glyph,
  soft glow; states normal / pressed / disabled / context-highlighted.
- Accessibility rows live in the settings card (text speed, subtitle size,
  colourblind-safe palettes, auto-advance, skip-hold).

## 6. Tone rules (from `Docs/Story/STORY_BIBLE.md` §2 — non-negotiable)

1. **Grief is shown through objects and verbs, never stated.** Nobody says
   "I miss her"; the action carries it.
2. **The horror erases, it never harms.** No blood, no threats of violence;
   the Shadow's menace is blank posters, half-remembered names, chalk-static
   voices.
3. **Hope-forward.** The night ends with knowledge carried out of a falling
   building; even the worst ending is quiet, not cruel.
4. **Restrained register.** Short sentences, concrete nouns: chalk, jasmine,
   red pen, crank flashlight, white sheets. No fantasy filler beyond the one
   symbol (Shadow = forgetting).
5. **The mother exists only in her own words** — margins, marks, sayings;
   warm, precise, a little formal, always about looking carefully.

Writing in any language follows these rules; Arabic keeps the same register
(elisions render as trailing dashes where the EN text leaves hollow gaps —
accepted deviation, STORY_BIBLE §10).
